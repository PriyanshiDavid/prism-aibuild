    For engineers: method and benchmarks

    Model. Squared Mahalanobis distance on five standardised operating variables (air temperature, process temperature, rotational speed, torque, tool wear). Fitted once on historical readings; no failure labels used to fit or score. Deterministic, with the whole model being five means, five standard deviations and a 5×5 matrix.

    Benchmark. Recorded failures found in each Top-N list (339 failures in 10,000 readings):

    Holdout. Fitted on half the data and scored on the unseen half (three stratified splits): Prism found the most failures at Top 50 and Top 500 in every split (mean 97 vs 64 for Isolation Forest V2 at Top 500 of 5,000).

    Explanations. Readings in the outer 5% of their range are named, most extreme first. Where no single reading is extreme, the variable with the largest conditional deviation given the others is named. For all 42 typed failures in the Top 100, the explanation names a physically linked variable.

    Limitations. One synthetic dataset; assumes a single normal operating mode; heat-dissipation and tool-wear failures are harder to catch from readings alone; random failures cannot be caught. Severity reflects unusualness, not business impact.

    Check your own data

    Prism works from a plain spreadsheet export: machines, deliveries, stock levels or purchase orders. No integration project, no data science team. Your file stays in this browser.

    What the file needs

    • A CSV with a header row and one row per record
    • At least two columns of numbers, for example distance, cost or time
    • Ideally 300 or more rows, so Prism can learn what's normal
    • Optional: a name or ID column
    • Optional: an importance or priority column (critical, VIP, high, standard, low, spare)

    Machine files with temperature, speed, torque and tool wear get Prism's machine wording and can be compared with the reference readings. Temperatures can be in °C or kelvin.

    Columns Prism is comparing

    Tap a column to leave it out. Prism re-ranks instantly.
